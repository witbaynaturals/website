About Our Cleaner

The Power of Orange & Vinegar

Witbay Naturals' all-purpose cleaner is made from simple ingredients: orange peels and white vinegar. The orange peels are rich in D-limonene, a powerful natural solvent that cuts through grease and grime, while the vinegar provides antibacterial properties and helps to dissolve mineral deposits.

What is D-limonene? Found in citrus fruit peels (up to 97% of the essential oils), D-limonene is one of the most common terpenes in nature. Research suggests it may offer anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial benefits. The FDA recognizes it as a safe food additive.

This powerful combination creates an effective, non-toxic cleaner that sanitizes surfaces, breaks down stubborn residues, and leaves a fresh citrus scent. Safe for use on countertops, appliances, floors, and more — no harsh chemicals, no fumes, just pure cleaning power.

Every bottle is handcrafted in small batches, using locally sourced orange peels and vinegar, packaged in reusable glass containers to minimize waste.

Instructions

How to use Witbay Naturals

01

Shake well

Always shake the bottle before use to ensure the natural oils are evenly distributed.

02

Spray onto surface

Spray directly onto the surface you wish to clean. For tough grime, let it sit for 30-60 seconds to allow the citrus oils to penetrate.

03

Wipe clean

Wipe clean with a clean cloth or sponge. For stubborn spots, use a scrub brush or non-scratch scouring pad.

04

No rinse needed

No rinsing required on most surfaces. Enjoy the fresh, clean citrus scent that naturally deodorizes as it cleans.
